Get to know La Michoacana Plus

Mexican Treats in Tulare

La Michoacana Plus bring Mexican frozen treats and snacks to Tulare, California. The shop offers paletas, ice cream, fruit desserts, aguas frescas, and savory snacks.

Sweet, Refreshing, and Savory

Mexican dessert shops offer a variety of ways to enjoy fruit, frozen treats, and snacks. At La Michoacana Plus, customers can explore options such as mangoneadas, fresas con crema, banana splits, and esquites.

A paleta is a frozen treat served on a stick. Aguas frescas are refreshing drinks, while fresas con crema combines strawberries with sweet cream.

What Makes the Shop Special

  • Colorful surroundings with Mexican decorations.
  • A menu with both sweet treats and savory snacks.
  • Fruit desserts and refreshing drink options.
  • A Tulare location for a casual visit with family or friends.
